About Chuandao AI

Using code as ink, AI as the edge, empowering enterprise digital transformation

Company Profile

Since its establishment in 2019, Chuandao AI has consistently anchored its core positioning of "using technology to solve business pain points and AI to activate growth potential." Starting as a startup team focused on software development, it has gradually grown into a digital solution service provider covering 18 core cities nationwide and serving over 300 enterprise clients.

We deeply understand that in the era of deep integration of AI and industry, "good software" is not just a collection of code, but a "digital engine" that fits business logic and carries strategic goals. Therefore, over the past five years, we have always been driven by the dual wheels of "technical depth + demand insight," continuously breaking through in the field of software development and helping enterprises cross the "last mile" of digital transformation.

Technical Strength & Team Building

Chuandao AI has built a core team of "practical + innovative" members, with over 120 current members, of which technical R&D personnel account for more than 70%, and 85% have more than 6 years of software development experience. The core team members all come from major internet companies and leading technology enterprises, having led the development of projects with tens of millions of users, such as financial risk control systems, intelligent manufacturing management platforms, and new retail data middle platforms.

Technical Achievements: We have always adhered to the concept of "technology feeding back to business," established a special R&D fund, and invested 20% of revenue annually in AI algorithm optimization, low-code platform iteration, and industry solution innovation. We have independently developed three core technical products: "Chuandao Low-Code Development Platform," "AI Data Cleaning Tool," and "Intelligent Business Process Engine," with a total of 42 software copyrights and 6 invention patents. Our technical strength has been certified by both ISO9001 Quality Management System and CMMI3 Software Capability Maturity Model.

Core Business & Service System

Chuandao AI focuses on the main line of "software development," forming three major business segments: "custom development + productized solutions + full-cycle operation and maintenance," covering digital needs across multiple industries and scenarios.

Customized Software Development

Targeting enterprise personalized needs, providing full-process services from requirement research, architecture design, code development to testing and launch, covering three major directions: enterprise management systems, industry-specific software, and mobile applications.

AI-Powered Solutions

Deeply integrating AI technology with industry scenarios, developing software systems with intelligent decision-making and automated processing capabilities, such as AI risk control audit systems, AI intelligent recommendation systems, etc.

Full-Cycle Technical Operation & Maintenance Services

Breaking the traditional model of "development as the endpoint," providing clients with 1-3 years of dedicated operation and maintenance services, including system bug fixes, feature iterations, performance optimization, data security assurance, etc.

Social Responsibility & Brand Philosophy

Chuandao AI has always practiced the values of "technology for good," closely integrating enterprise development with social value.

  • 2021 Launched "AI Enterprise Assistance Public Welfare Program": Provided free basic management software development and digital consulting services to 15 small and micro enterprises
  • 2023 Jointly launched "Chuandao AI Technology Training Camp" with universities: Cumulatively trained over 500 software development talents with practical capabilities

Future Outlook

Looking to the future, Chuandao AI will continue to build on "software development" as its foundation and "AI technology innovation" as its breakthrough point, planning to launch an "AI Lightweight Management Suite" for small and medium enterprises in 2025 to reduce digital transformation costs; while expanding into new tracks such as industrial software and cross-border e-commerce systems, creating more industry benchmark projects.

We always remember that "Chuandao" is not just a brand name, but our original intention - to not only deliver cutting-edge technology and solutions, but also to convey the concept of "serving business with technology," working hand in hand with every client to grow together in the digital wave, using code and AI to write a new chapter in enterprise development.
